# Broad Polymarket build handover — 14 October 2026

1. Scope: independent broad-market paper agent, no changes to existing BTC or
   company research services, no trading-wallet access or financial execution.
2. Data: public GET endpoints only; catalogue pages are bounded and rotating.
   Exact contract rules, assets, source deadlines, fees and timestamps retained.
3. Strategies: complementary pairs evaluated after depth and fees; maker watch
   has no assumed fills/rewards; reviewed cross-market matrices diagnostic only.
4. Capital: $100 virtual initial cash, <=$5 per entry, <=$20 open cost, persistent
   positions, unique market entries and explicit official settlement.
5. Execution: two-second delayed paper recheck; sequential book observations
   are not atomic fills and never count as real trading evidence.
6. Model: prospectively registered quote-risk logistic study. Contract-disjoint
   chronological phases, purging, calibration, hashed test predictions and
   2,000 bootstrap resamples. No model promotion or demonstrated trading edge.
7. Validation: 25 isolated tests locally/on server; synthetic study exercises
   training only on generated data. Public integration captured nine supported
   fresh books in the final 20-market scan. No qualified paper pair observed.
8. Operations: standard-library scanner, bounded storage, private state, STOP
   flag, writer lock, status and chained journal; paper-service installer ready.
   Persistent service installation remains pending owner root operation.
9. Limitations: v1 ordinary binaries only; V2, negative-risk and matching delays
   excluded. Polling is not HFT. Live paired executor not implemented because
   equal-share FOK/settlement/partial-leg handling is not validated. Official
   SDK 0.13.0 signatures inspected without signing/account calls. Eligibility
   check reported eligible; account setup and actual fills remain unverified.
10. Next gates: install reviewed paper service, collect 14 days and sufficient
    distinct-contract labels, evaluate quote-risk model, separately validate
    executable live strategy and privately configure eligible owner account.
    Do not represent this build as a trained profitable live trader.
